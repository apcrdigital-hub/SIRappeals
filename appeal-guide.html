Why First File Online, Then Generate Detailed Legal Appeal on the Offline Application Draft Builder page?

In Special Intensive Revision (SIR) proceedings, an aggrieved voter whose name has been wrongly deleted or flagged has a strict 15-day statutory window to file an appeal before the Designated Appellate Authority (District Magistrate / Additional District Magistrate / Sub-Divisional Officer) under Section 24 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950.

Based on APCR's on-the-ground interventions across states, relying exclusively on either the online portal or a standalone physical submission creates significant procedural risks. The most effective legal recourse is the Two-Step Protocol:

The Dual-Channel Strategy:
1. Online Filing on ECINet (voters.eci.gov.in): Creates an official, timestamped electronic entry with an Application Acknowledgment Number under the direct supervisory radar of the ECI and the Supreme Court.
2. Crucial Online Portal Limitation: The online portal restricts "Brief Facts" to 1,000 characters, "Relief Sought" to 500 characters, and strictly does NOT permit uploading any documentary proofs or annexures.
3. Comprehensive Physical Representation (Offline Application Draft Builder page): By entering the online reference number into our Offline Application Draft Builder, the elector generates an exhaustive legal petition citing Supreme Court precedents (Lal Babu Hussein, ADR v. ECI), natural justice violations, and an itemized schedule of verified documents, submitted directly to the Appellate Authority/Dropbox.
1

File Initial Appeal Online

Visit voters.eci.gov.in → Click 'Submit Appeal for Individuals' → Authenticate via OTP & Aadhaar E-Hastakshar → Obtain the Online Reference / Acknowledgment Number.

2

Open the Offline Application Draft Builder page → Select "Yes, Online Appeal Submitted" → Enter your Reference Number → Select your specific scenario (1–13) → Download full legal petition with legal grounds.

3

Submit Evidence Package

Attach the online acknowledgment slip + detailed petition from the Offline Application Draft Builder page + certified copies of all proofs → Deposit at nearest designated Appellate Authority Dropbox / SDM / DM office.

The Critical Portal Limitation: Why Online Filing Alone Is Insufficient

Warning: The Online Portal Does NOT Allow Uploading Documents!

The official ECI portal was designed with structural constraints:
  • Zero Document Uploads: There is no feature on the online portal to attach or upload PDF, JPG, or any digital copies of Aadhaar, Birth Certificates, School Certificates, Land Records, or 2002 SIR Roll baseline sheets.
  • Character Caps: Brief facts are capped at 1,000 characters and Relief at 500 characters, making it impossible to lay out comprehensive legal grounds, natural justice violations, or case laws.
  • Risk of Summary Rejection: An appeal without supporting physical documentary evidence cannot satisfy the legal standard of proof before the Appellate Tribunal.

Under the Indian Evidence Act and the Representation of the People Act, 1950, proceedings before the Appellate Officer (DM / ADM / SDM) are quasi-judicial civil proceedings. The standard of proof is the preponderance of probabilities. To succeed, the appellant must place a complete evidentiary dossier on record. This is why APCR developed the Offline Application Draft Builder page.

Assembling the Complete Evidence Package & Dropbox Submission

The Volunteer Handbook sets out the physical submission protocol. Once the appeal is filed online and the detailed petition is printed from the Offline Application Draft Builder page, assemble the final package in this order:

Checklist of Documents to Staple Together:

  • Page 1: Printout of the Online Appeal Acknowledgment Slip (downloaded from voters.eci.gov.in showing the Application Reference Number).
  • Pages 2–4: The Detailed Legal Representation generated from the Offline Application Draft Builder page (bearing the elector's signature/thumb impression and verification date).
  • Annexure A: Self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card (for identity/address verification, as affirmed by SC in ADR v. ECI).
  • Annexure B: Primary citizenship/birth proof (Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation / Gram Panchayat, or School Transfer Certificate / Matriculation Certificate).
  • Annexure C: Legacy / Linkage records (Excerpts of 2002 SIR Electoral Roll showing parents'/grandparents' inclusion).
  • Annexure D: Certified copy of the Impugned Deletion Order / Notice (if served), or a specific declaration that no notice was ever served.
Where to Submit: Deposit the sealed dossier at the nearest designated Appellate Authority Dropbox (District Election Office / Deputy Commissioner / Assistant Commissioner / Sub-Divisional Magistrate office). Obtain an acknowledgment receipt / receiving stamp on an office copy for your records.

Key Legal Standards for Volunteers

Standard of Proof: Preponderance of Probabilities

This is a civil proceeding, not a criminal trial. The standard is preponderance of probabilities—the appellant does not have to prove their citizenship or linkage beyond a reasonable doubt, but simply establish that their eligibility is more probable than not based on the cumulative record.

Linkage vs. Core Qualification

Settled Law: Mere failure to establish an algorithmic or mechanical linkage to a pre-existing 2002 entry cannot be the sole basis for disenfranchisement if the elector independently proves core constitutional qualifications: (1) Indian Citizenship, (2) 18+ Years of Age, and (3) Ordinary Residence.
