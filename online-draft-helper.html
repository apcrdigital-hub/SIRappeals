⚠️ MANDATORY TWO-STEP APPEAL PROCEDURE (ONLINE FIRST, THEN PHYSICAL SUBMISSION)

Under the Election Commission of India (ECI) framework, filing an appeal against SIR electoral deletions/notices requires a two-step dual-channel process. Follow this sequence strictly:

STEP 1: ONLINE FILING File on voters.eci.gov.in

The ECI online portal caps Brief Facts at 1,000 characters, Relief Sought at 500 characters, and does not permit uploading documentary evidence. Use this helper page to generate compliant concise text, paste it into the portal, submit, and record your Online Appeal Reference Number.

STEP 2: PHYSICAL SUBMISSION Submit via Offline Application Draft Builder

Open our Offline Application Draft Builder, enter your Online Reference Number, and download the full legal petition. The downloaded application must be printed, signed, and physically submitted along with certified/self-attested copies of all documents mentioned in the appeal to the Appellate Authority / Dropbox (DM/SDM office).

Notice: An online appeal alone without physical submission of documentary proofs is incomplete because the online portal does not accept attachments. Both steps are mandatory.
